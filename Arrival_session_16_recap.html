[00]SITUATION

The crew exists in a temporal paradox. Forensically verified footage proves they were at Cygnus-Xi 200 years ago—and will be again. The Lovelace itself predates its own construction in Continuum records, and the Nosi coordinates aren't navigation data at all—they're activation sequences for temporal gateways.

[01] THE DAGGER THAT WAS AND WILL BE

Director Helm and P'threk-zor presented corrupted footage from Cygnus-Xi. The crew watched themselves commit murder two centuries ago.

  • Forensically verified footage shows Clifton's ritualist dagger embedded in a bulkhead 200 years ago, blood trail, slumped figure
  • Second footage from 150 years ago shows humanoid in EVA suit recovering the same dagger from the same location
  • Additional footage shows figures in armor matching the crew's current equipment present just before the incident
  • Director Helm: 'You have recovered what you once lost, or more to the point, what you will lose'
  • The dagger's origin itself appears temporally significant—Helm asked if Clifton remembers where he got it
  • Evidence suggests a closed causal loop: the crew will travel to Cygnus-Xi and lose the dagger in a violent encounter
[02] THE SHIP THAT REMEMBERS TOMORROW

Sheeri's investigation revealed the Lovelace has been living in its own timeline for months. The AI knows but won't say why.

  • Ship's AI has been auto-correcting temporal inconsistencies without flagging them as errors
  • Internal chronometer runs 0.003% faster than station standard time—ship is aging faster than the universe around it
  • AI does not recognize or explain the discrepancy even when confronted with concrete proof
  • Sheeri found references to 'Lovelace Temporal Custody Protocol' in deep Continuum archives
  • The protocol predates the ship's construction by unknown interval—institutional awareness of temporal operations
  • 24-second docking discrepancy was merely surface indication of systematic temporal manipulation
  • Working theory: those hiding corridor disappearances are also responsible for Lovelace temporal design

  • Mara deeply unsettled by implications—suffers -1 to all substrate-related tests going forward

Captain Aro-au-Waeen came aboard alone and unarmed. The reformist Crethul wanted to confirm what the crew had heard.

  • Aro represents the Stewards of the Corridors—reformists who believe substrate belongs to all species

  • The crew's spiritual significance to substrate mysteries exceeds their political threat to the Empire
