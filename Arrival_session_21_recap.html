[00]SITUATION

The crew arrived at Halvern's Reach to search for the Nosi waystones. Instead they found a derelict Crethul Guardian-class ship with one life sign aboard. That life sign was the Amalgam—a metallic plant organism that had absorbed hundreds of Crethul crew into its structure.

[01] THE DERELICT

The Guardian-class vessel hung dead in space. No power, no atmosphere, intact hull. One life sign.

  • Guardian-class ships patrol substrate corridors, typically carrying ~200 Crethul crew including apostolate, scientists, castigators
  • Neyla's sensors detected only a dozen or two dozen escape pods used—not nearly enough for full crew complement
  • No bodies, no skeletons, no remains visible during initial approach
  • Ship appeared abandoned but structurally sound
[02] THE AMALGAM

Inside engineering, the crew found the source of the life sign. A metallic plant-like organism suspended by its own tendrils.

  • Central node held in suspension by cable-like tendrils woven throughout ship's systems
  • Hundreds of Crethul faces visible on the node's surface, mouthing wordlessly
  • Organism exhibited intelligence and defensive behavior when crew attempted to cut through its cables
  • Elias suffered bruised ribs when tendrils constricted after plasma cutter contact
  • Mara was pierced through suit and flesh by tendril during escape attempt—major injury
  • Kepler appeared suddenly using stealth abilities and killed the creature with sneak attack rifle fire
  • Amalgam died instantly when central node destroyed. No clear explanation for what created it or how the crew became it
[03] SELF-DESTRUCT

Restoring emergency power to access ship systems triggered an automatic sequence. The crew had minutes.

  • Nuclear self-destruct activated when power restored—standard Guardian-class protocol for religious contamination scenarios
  • Crew attempted emergency teleport evacuation coordinated by Rep K
  • Rep K and Clifton's teleport catastrophically failed—materialized in open space behind the exploding ship
  • Rep K used Compel to mentally control Clifton and force correct teleportation to quad walker
  • Mental control succeeded but Rep K suffered mental debility as backlash from stilted execution
  • Clifton broke Rep K's backup shock staff using it as crowbar to manually open airlock when teleport failed
[04] RESCUE

Two crew members stranded in space. Nuclear explosion imminent. Quad walker their only hope.

  • Elias commanded Rep K's Formicide crew to pilot quad walker for rescue despite their reluctance to approach detonation
  • Crew surfed the explosion's shockwave to reach Rep K and Clifton faster
  • Successfully retrieved both crew members before ship's complete destruction
  • Quad walker suffered severe damage from shockwave—now nonfunctional and adrift
  • Crethul vessel completely destroyed in nuclear explosion—highly visible event in Halvern's Reach system
  • Lovelace maneuvered to safe distance and intercepted damaged walker
[05] AFTERMATH

The crew returned to the Lovelace. Equipment destroyed. Crew injured. No answers about what happened to the Guardian ship.

  • Mara's major injury from tendril piercing requires treatment
  • Rep K's mental debility from forced Compel attempt needs recovery time
  • Clifton continues struggling with Rep K's Formicide body—extra limbs, wings, unfamiliar neurology
  • Quad walker transport completely destroyed—significant loss of crew capabilities
  • Nuclear explosion may draw attention to crew's presence at Halvern's Reach
  • Still no progress on finding the Nosi waystones—the actual mission objective
  • No explanation for how hundreds of Crethul became a single metallic plant organism
