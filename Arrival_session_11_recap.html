[00]SITUATION

The crew diverted from their Nosi rendezvous to answer an eleven-day-old distress call at abandoned Cygnus-Xi Station. They found Sable Ynnsate, a stranded data scavenger with a damaged ship and 200+ years of survey data to trade. What began as simple negotiations turned chaotic when Benedict emerged from Kepler's pants and sprayed acid across the crew.

[01] DISTRESS DETOUR

The crew spent a week in transit researching Crethul politics and debating their next move. An old distress signal from Cygnus-Xi offered a potential side job.

  • Mara researched Mena-ei-Beloli, a tolerant Crethul voice who argued substrate corridors belong to all species—possible leverage against the Empress
  • Picked up an eleven-day-old distress signal from abandoned station Cygnus-Xi, derelict for over 200 years
  • Station appeared deliberately breached at multiple points, defended by hundreds of repurposed sensor-scrambling drones
  • Docking complicated by parasitic drone attachment, leaving incomplete seal requiring EVA suits for access
[02] SABLE YNNSATE

The distress caller turned out to be a solo data scavenger with a broken ship and valuable cargo. Negotiations began smoothly.

  • Met Sable Ynnsate, data scavenger stranded aboard her ship Secondhand Reckoning with damaged antimatter intake
  • Sable offered 200+ years of corrupted survey data in exchange for repairs—personal use only, not resale
  • Data includes outdated corridor charts, potentially revealing unmapped regions or vanished routes
  • Kepler appeared romantically stunned during first contact
[03] BENEDICT EMERGES

During a station tour, the creature living in Kepler's pants made a dramatic and acidic introduction.

  • Benedict emerged from Kepler's pants in front of Sable and the crew
  • When separated from polymer sealant food source, Benedict sprayed highly acidic compound at the crew
  • Kepler took acid burns to the right side of face, Mara got shoved into cleaning supplies during the chaos
  • Sable calmed Benedict with specific sounds and gestures, revealing familiarity with the species
[04] JELLYMORPHS

Sable identified Benedict's species and explained their nature. The crew learned what they've been carrying.

  • Sable calls the species 'jellymorphs'—genderless polymer-feeding organisms about the size of a shih tzu
  • They produce acid when threatened or hungry, can mimic hand gestures, and respond to specific calming techniques
  • Cygnus-Xi station contains polymer sealant that jellymorphs feed on, explaining Benedict's interest
  • Kepler began researching communication methods with Benedict while nursing facial burns
[05] THE STATION'S SECRETS

Cygnus-Xi holds more than data. The crew started to piece together why it was abandoned.

  • Station deliberately breached at multiple points over 200 years ago—personal logs around the incident heavily corrupted
  • Hundreds of repurposed drones scramble sensors and block short-range communications, making exploration hazardous
  • Workshop, medbay, archives, and storage remain accessible on minimal reactor power
  • No clear explanation yet for what happened or why the station was systematically opened to space
