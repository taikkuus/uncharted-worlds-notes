[00]SITUATION

The Lovelace limps toward Verath Ring Station with a captured Queldrath salvage vessel in tow and four robot prisoners locked in the cargo hold. Both ships suffered catastrophic damage in the firefight. The crew survived their first piracy attempt, but questions remain about how the Queldrath knew exactly what cargo to demand.

[01] NEW HIRE

The crew formalized Sable Ynnsate's employment contract despite clear evidence her identity is fabricated.

  • Sable signed on as crew member after losing everything in the Secondhand Reckoning explosion
  • Background check revealed identity inconsistencies that crew chose not to press
  • She brings data scavenging expertise and station knowledge to operations
  • Kepler providing emotional support as Sable processes ship loss
[02] PIRACY ATTEMPT

A Queldrath salvage vessel twice the Lovelace's size made aggressive contact and demanded cargo transfer.

  • Queldrath specifically targeted Nosi-acquired materials: holographic projector, void motes, exotic matter, substrate resonant materials, builder era technology
  • They possessed detailed cargo scan data despite no previous contact
  • Undesignated Ninth Captain delivered ultimatum: transfer cargo or face extraction
  • Crew refused surrender and prepared for combat
[03] SHIP-TO-SHIP ENGAGEMENT

The Lovelace engaged in brutal close-quarters combat with the larger Queldrath vessel.

  • Multiple exchanges of plasma and energy cannon fire devastated both ships
  • The Loudmouth overheated from repeated firing during extended engagement
  • Lovelace suffered severe engineering damage and minor crew quarters damage
  • Queldrath vessel lost helm integrity, quarters destroyed, engineering crippled
  • Undesignated First killed in hull breach during firefight
  • Undesignated Ninth Captain surrendered after losing vessel control
[04] PRISONERS SECURED

Four Queldrath robots now occupy the cargo hold under guard while their disabled ship trails behind.

  • Crew disabled Queldrath weapons systems before securing prisoners
  • Undesignated Ninth Captain and three crew members (including Designated 37) confined to cargo hold
  • Benedict kept separated from prisoners due to potential material consumption conflict
  • Queldrath vessel being towed contains Class 1 cargo: enriched carbon, mechanical components, power cells, decommissioned circuit boards
  • Full combat records preserved showing clear self-defense scenario
[05] VERATH BOUND

The damaged Lovelace continues toward Verath Ring Station with multiple objectives converging.

  • Crew plans to turn Queldrath prisoners over to Continuum law enforcement
  • Verath represents best opportunity to investigate substrate dissolution cover-ups at the heart of Continuum space
  • Station predates the Continuum by over a billion years—original builders unknown
  • Kepler researching which administrative entities are systematically canceling substrate investigations
  • Several days travel remaining to reach the ring megastructure
