[00]SITUATION

The crew fled Verath Station during an Imperial diplomatic quarantine, rejecting the Empress's offer to dissolve the Sacred Reckoning in exchange for surrender. Neyla piloted recklessly past the Imperial command shuttle carrying a VIP. Multiple factions are extremely angry.

[01] THE TREVOS ESCALATION

Finch's panic about the investigation turned into kidnapping. Mara helped him escape despite telling him she's done.

  • Finch kidnapped Alyssa, Trevos's older sister who was following him on Verath Station, and sedated her with an auto doc
  • Mara arranged fake credentials through Rep K's criminal network—not foolproof but should provide a few days head start
  • Finch successfully escaped the station before the Lovelace departed, wiping all communication records with a data worm
  • Rep K's criminal network is now blown on Verath Station as consequence of creating the fake ID
  • Mara told Finch she's done covering for his mistakes, but their relationship remains strained and unresolved
[02] JORDETH'S INTELLIGENCE

Kepler met with information broker Jordeth. The exchange revealed corporate conspiracy and institutional foreknowledge of the crew's temporal fate.

  • Coldwell Holdings hired the Queldrith as contractors to attack the Lovelace at Phoenix 27C node one week before the attack
  • Coldwell Holdings has Queldrith searching abandoned nodes for Nosi technology
  • Someone in the Continuum is leaking data to Coldwell Holdings—inside job confirmed in the disappearing systems cover-up
  • Jordeth provided audio clip: Director Helm and P'threk-zor discussing the crew's inevitable time travel, potential memory loss, paradox psychosis, and unstable temporal loops
  • Kepler's failed poker face when Jordeth mentioned Lovelace Temporal Custody Protocol Article Seven revealed crew knowledge of closed timelike curves
  • Article Seven translation: 'Objects from closed timelike curves must be returned to custodians at predetermined intervals to maintain causal stability'—crew must put things back when/where found
  • Jordeth can sit on the new information for a few days out of professional courtesy, then will sell to other buyers
[03] THE EMPRESS'S OFFER

Empress Ola-owa-Namen arrived personally with the Imperial Flagship. The offer was specific and unsettling.

  • Empress called the crew 'those who walk between times,' claiming to know what they carry and where they flee
  • Offered to dissolve the Sacred Reckoning and provide Imperial protection if crew surrendered by boarding the flagship
  • Warning delivered: 'Nosi cannot protect you from what hunts' but 'the eternal flame can'
  • Arrival triggered diplomatic quarantine of entire station sector with no departures permitted
  • Implied something beyond Crethul religious authority is pursuing the crew—something even Nosi technology cannot defend against
[04] ESCAPE FROM VERATH

The crew chose to run rather than surrender. The escape was expensive and reckless.

  • Elias bribed station officials with Class IV cargo to arrange technical malfunction releasing docking clamps and delete extradition paperwork
  • Officials timed the release for when Crethul ships transferred to station control, allowing normal jump instead of wild jump
  • Neyla piloted recklessly, buzzing the Imperial command shuttle carrying a VIP before hitting the substrate node
  • Crew successfully escaped to substrate transit using the Nosi temporal coordinates
  • Crew debt to Crethul Empire increased from one to three total
  • Multiple factions generated significant angry communications chatter during departure
[05] SHEERI'S BREAKTHROUGH

Elias's assistant worked 38 hours straight on caffeine and stimulants. Her findings connect everything.

  • Disappeared systems, Nosi coordinates, and Lovelace time drift all share identical harmonic signature—they sync perfectly when overlapped
  • Conclusion: 'Whatever makes star systems vanish is also making our clocks run fast'
  • Someone knew the Lovelace's temporal nature when the ship was built—'Ship was built for that'
  • Produced 15 hours of dictated notes—early portions coherent and valuable, later portions increasingly circular and incoherent
  • Ordered by Elias to write everything down, take massive sedative, and sleep for a day
  • Insists on staying with crew rather than remaining on station: 'I am coming with you and I am gonna see this through, period'
