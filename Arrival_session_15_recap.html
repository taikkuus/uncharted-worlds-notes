[01] HANGOVERS AND HALLUCINATIONS

The morning after Neyla's birthday brought consequences. Royal jelly caused substrate-related hallucinations for non-formicides.

  • Rep K unaffected due to formicide biology—multiple stomachs filtered the intoxicant by morning
  • Neyla and Mara remained clear-headed throughout, not susceptible to the formicide substance
  • Clifton, Elias, and Kepler nursed hangovers; Kepler attempted hair-of-the-dog cure
  • Softgold-Pulse-Steady arrived aboard with Deepviolet-spiral-2, a dancer from Parahelion, as his companion
[02] TEMPORAL ANOMALY

Station security discovered the Lovelace's docking records show impossible timing. The ship arrived 24 seconds before it actually docked.

  • Security officer Thesslin documented the discrepancy but attributed it to calibration error
  • The anomaly appears isolated to the Lovelace—no other vessels show similar irregularities
  • Elias and Sheeri began cross-referencing timestamps across multiple systems to identify the source
  • Potentially connected to 200-year-old Lovelace logs found at Cygnus-Xi
  • Elias implemented enhanced security protocols requiring passwords at docking doors
[03] THE SCHOOL'S REACH

Softgold-Pulse-Steady revealed uncomfortable truths about his recruitment and Director Helm's intelligence network.

  • Director Helm told Softgold about the Nosi coordinates before he joined the crew—information the crew never shared with the School
  • Softgold admits he's reporting back to Helm, serving dual purposes as researcher and School operative
  • Rep K questioned Softgold about loyalty; Softgold expressed hope he'd never need the shock staff given to him
  • Softgold mentioned Clifton showed great talent at the School and was requested to stay, but chose military service instead
  • Director Helm was disappointed by Clifton's choice—the ritualist dagger connection remains unexplained
[04] CRETHUL NEIGHBORS

A rare Crethul merchant frigate docked nearby. Merchant vessels are extremely uncommon among the theocratic empire.

  • Clifton spotted the ship immediately after leaving for breakfast and alerted the crew
  • Elias investigated remotely but was detected by the ship's owners
  • The vessel carries approximately a dozen crew seeking cargo buyers—legitimate commerce rather than religious mission
  • Crew debated approach versus avoidance given the unresolved Sacred Reckoning hanging over them
  • Mara and Neyla ran diagnostics as precaution; no hostile action detected
[05] TREVOS AND JORDETH

Two separate intelligence threads emerged. Mara learned someone is investigating a covered-up death. Sable offered to connect Kepler with information broker Jordeth.

  • Finch met with Mara at Parahelion to warn her someone is asking about Trevos—a teenager they accidentally killed during an extraction job years ago
  • They covered up the death out of panic despite following protocol; the investigation trail appears warm
  • Finch showed Mara pictures of a Rethani woman conducting inquiries; Finch is panicked but hasn't contacted anyone else
  • Sable offered to introduce Kepler to Jordeth in exchange for Cygnus-Xi archive data and details about what the Queldrith detected
  • Jordeth believes the Queldrith attack and substrate dissolution investigations are related; wants complete data in exchange for intelligence
