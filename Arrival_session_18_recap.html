[00]SITUATION

The Lovelace used Nosi coordinates to attempt temporal navigation and was pulled through substrate by a massive worm-like entity composed of distorted spacetime. After seven rhythmic energy pulses that decreased in strength, the ship was expelled into normal space over 200 years in the past. The crew has arrived at Cygnus-Xi station at the exact moment their own historical logs were filed.

[01] TEMPORAL DESCENT

The crew activated Nosi coordinates as temporal entry sequences. The substrate did not respond well.

  • Catastrophic substrate instabilities immediately manifested - tunnel walls warping, energy cascades forming
  • Temporal ghosts began appearing throughout the ship - alternate quantum possibilities made briefly real
  • One ghost manifestation of a Crethul castigator critically injured Rep K, frying his substrate connection in his brain
  • Ghosts became faster and more numerous as transit progressed - thousands of choices being made elsewhere across possibilities
  • Neyla's emergency maneuvering caused ship to shear against tunnel wall, tearing a rift in substrate fabric
[02] THE TIME WORM

A massive entity emerged from the rift the Lovelace created. Not biological - composed of distorted space itself.

  • Interior showed distorted stars and galaxies instead of organic tissue when visible
  • Entity pulled the Lovelace back into the substrate despite Neyla's attempts to escape
  • Seven rhythmic energy pulses marked the descent - each decreasing in strength
  • Mara successfully decoded the pattern and predicted the seventh pulse would expel the ship
  • Connection to substrate enforcement or temporal correction remains unclear
[03] DAMAGE CONTROL

The ship took severe punishment during temporal transit. Crew worked to prevent total loss.

  • Helm sustained major damage from earlier Crethul energy cannon strike
  • Multiple fires erupted throughout crew deck - Clifton used elemental manipulation to force flames into airlocks
  • Hull breaches manifested - both real damage and temporal ghost possibilities
  • Clifton directed crew to jury-rig temporal stabilizers using emergency bulkheads
  • One of Rep K's assistant teams suffered ongoing penalties from panic and ghost attacks
  • Elias sustained major burns, Mara suffered severe nosebleed from substrate exposure
[04] ARRIVAL IN THE PAST

The Lovelace emerged from substrate over 200 years before the crew's original timeline. Cygnus-Xi station sits intact before them.

  • Neyla calculated initial arrival at 25 years in past, then recalculated to over 200 years
  • Station shows approximately 20 ships currently docked - operational and undamaged
  • This is the exact moment when the Lovelace's historical logs were filed at the station
  • The crew is now positioned to witness or participate in events that created their temporal paradox
  • The murder involving Clifton's dagger, the data deletion, and the station attack all lie ahead in this timeline
[05] THE CLOSED LOOP

The crew must now navigate their role in maintaining temporal stability while critically damaged. Article Seven of the Lovelace Temporal Custody Protocol is no longer theoretical.

  • Objects from closed timelike curves must be returned to custodians at predetermined intervals
  • The crew must complete the murder/retrieval cycle involving Clifton's dagger to prevent causal breakdown
  • Director Helm's warning about memory loss, paradox psychosis, and unstable temporal loops now applies
  • The question remains whether the crew acts knowingly or as puppets in predetermined events
  • Ship requires extensive repairs in an era where proper parts and medical facilities may not exist for their timeline
