[00]SITUATION

The Lovelace sits damaged at Emergency Dock 13 while Rep K undergoes emergency brain surgery and Kiva awaits treatment for catastrophic temporal fusion injuries. A young Mena-ei-Beloli has confirmed what the crew suspected: they must recover the Nosi waystones and return them to complete the closed time loop before the young Empress—already aware of their arrival—comes for them. The crew has entered the paradox they've been running from.

[01] EMERGENCE

The Lovelace tore through 200 years of timeline and emerged over Cygnus-Xi trailing fire and structural damage. Commander Arvellin offered Emergency Dock 13 with full medical support.

  • Ship suffered unprecedented hull shearing damage from scraping substrate corridor edges during emergency maneuver that summoned the time worm
  • Hull shows molecular-level fusion with foreign material—harmonic frequency mismatches indicate damage to the substrate corridor itself
  • Station engineer Tolvin investigating damage he's never seen before; crew told him 'wild jump went bad' but avoiding specifics
  • All interior door locks forced open to reach Kiva's quarters; station documentation will record this unusual incident
[02] CASUALTIES

Temporal transit extracted a severe price. Two crew members require surgery for injuries that station medics have never treated before.

  • Rep K underwent emergency brain surgery for substrate-related neural damage—neural harmonics out of sync with baseline frequencies
  • Requires second surgery after swelling reduces, then substrate harmonic meditation regimen to realign frequencies
  • Cannot safely use substrate abilities except clumsily with significant risk; further substrate travel could reverse surgical progress
  • Kiva suffered catastrophic temporal fusion: duplicate organs, extra fingers, two pupils in one eye, left leg fused with ship's bulkhead at molecular level
  • Mara used plasma cutter to free Kiva from bulkhead without injury; auto-doc stabilized her but professional surgery required for duplicate organs
  • Other crew injuries treated successfully and will heal within hours
[03] THE HERETIC

A young Crethul woman contacted the crew requesting a meeting at closed Docking Bay 7. Four crew members infiltrated to meet her, leaving physical evidence behind.

  • Mena-ei-Beloli identified herself as future heretic and spiritual leader of the Stewards of the Corridors—not yet accused but knows she will be imprisoned
  • Crethul gain substrate memories of all future transits; she remembers meeting the crew from her future perspective
  • Confirmed crew is at center of closed time loop and must return Nosi waystones to where they were found to prevent paradoxes
  • System disappearances in crew's native timeline are likely linked to temporal paradoxes from incomplete time loops
  • Warned crew must not attempt to change the future—doing so could fracture their psyches and destroy entire systems or galaxies
  • Stated young Empress (currently a child with extensive substrate memories) likely knows crew has arrived and will be coming
  • Empress believes herself master of substrate and opposes anyone else claiming such mastery; nothing crew does will change her conviction
  • Advised crew to avoid the Empress, recover the waystones 'the hard way' from the Nosi, and not delay
  • Believes this very conversation may be the evidence used to accuse her of heresy in the future
[04] THE MISSION

The crew's path forward is now clear, if not simple. They must recover objects they already delivered and return them to a place they already found them.

  • Must recover seven Nosi waystones from the Nosi who currently possess them—cannot simply travel back before initial retrieval
  • Must return waystones to Tezzek Null where they were originally found with the dead Nosi to complete the closed time loop
  • Lovelace Temporal Custody Protocol Article Seven mandates this: objects from closed timelike curves must be returned to maintain causal stability
  • Failure to complete the loop may be causing or contributing to system disappearances in the crew's native timeline
  • The Empire acquiring the waystones would exacerbate the paradox situation
  • Crew must act before the young Empress arrives at Cygnus-Xi
[05] THE CLOCK

Time is not on the crew's side. Multiple pressures converge on their damaged ship docked at a station 200 years in the past.

  • Rep K requires second surgery and extended recovery before substrate travel is medically advisable
  • Kiva requires professional surgery to address duplicate organs from temporal fusion
  • Ship damage requires assessment and potentially extended repairs before safe transit
  • Young Empress likely already aware of crew's arrival and coming to Cygnus-Xi
  • Station engineer and medical staff documenting unprecedented injuries and damage that could draw attention
  • Crew left physical evidence at Docking Bay 7 during infiltration to meet Mena-ei-Beloli
  • Humans not yet part of Continuum in this time period—human crew members and artifacts could raise questions
